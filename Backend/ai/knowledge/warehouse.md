# EMART Warehouse Services

## Our Warehouse

EMART operates a secure warehouse facility in Japan where all purchased items are received, processed, and stored before international shipping.

**Location**: Tokyo, Japan (specific address provided to registered users)

## Warehouse Services

### Package Receiving
- We receive packages from all Japanese sellers and marketplaces
- Each package is logged into your account within 24 hours of arrival
- You receive email notification when items arrive
- All packages are scanned and photographed upon receipt

### Free Storage Period
- **60 days free storage** from the date your first item arrives
- No storage fees for the first 60 days
- Secure, climate-controlled environment
- 24/7 security monitoring

### Extended Storage
If you need to store items longer than 60 days:
- **Days 61-90**: ¥100 per day
- **Days 91-120**: ¥200 per day
- **After 120 days**: Contact customer support for long-term storage options

### Package Consolidation
Save on international shipping by consolidating multiple purchases:
- **Free consolidation** for up to 5 items
- ¥200 for each additional item beyond 5
- Consolidation typically completed within 1-2 business days
- All items purchased within 60 days can be consolidated

### Photo Inspection Service
Request photos of your items before shipping:
- **Basic Inspection**: ¥200 - Includes 3-5 clear photos of the package and contents
- **Detailed Inspection**: ¥500 - Includes 10+ photos from multiple angles, close-ups of any concerns
- Photos uploaded to your dashboard within 24 hours

What we check:
- Item condition
- Completeness (all parts/accessories included)
- Packaging quality
- Any visible damage
- Authenticity verification (for branded items)

### Repackaging Service
Reduce shipping costs by optimizing package size and weight:

**Basic Repackaging** - ¥300
- Remove unnecessary outer boxes
- Use smaller box if possible
- Add protective padding as needed

**Advanced Repackaging** - ¥600
- Remove all retail packaging
- Consolidate items efficiently
- Minimize dimensions and weight
- Add custom protective packaging
- Can reduce volume by up to 50%

### Special Handling
For fragile or valuable items:
- Extra protective packaging available
- Insurance options up to ¥200,000
- Temperature-controlled storage for sensitive items
- Separate handling for high-value packages

## Warehouse Processing Times

- **Package Receipt**: Items logged within 24 hours of arrival
- **Photo Inspection**: Completed within 24 hours of request
- **Repackaging**: Completed within 1-2 business days
- **Consolidation**: Completed within 1-2 business days
- **Shipping Preparation**: Packages shipped within 1-2 business days after payment

## Your Warehouse Dashboard

Track all your items in real-time through your EMART account:
- View all items currently in warehouse
- See arrival dates and storage time remaining
- Request services (photos, repackaging, consolidation)
- Choose shipping method and destination
- View package photos and inspection reports
- Track storage fees (if applicable)

## Security and Safety

- 24/7 CCTV monitoring
- Secure access control
- Climate-controlled environment (temperature and humidity)
- Fire suppression system
- Insurance coverage for all stored items
- Regular inventory audits

## Package Identification

Each package is assigned a unique warehouse ID:
- Format: EMW-XXXXXX
- Linked to your account automatically
- Used for all tracking and service requests
- QR code generated for easy mobile access

## Abandonment Policy

Packages unclaimed after 120 days:
- We attempt to contact you via email (3 times)
- After 120 days + 30 days notice, items may be disposed of
- Storage fees apply for entire period
- We are not liable for abandoned packages

## Prohibited Items

Our warehouse cannot accept:
- Illegal items in Japan
- Dangerous goods (explosives, chemicals)
- Live animals or plants
- Perishable foods (unless frozen and pre-arranged)
- Items over 30kg (special handling required)
- Oversized items over 160cm total dimensions (special handling required)
