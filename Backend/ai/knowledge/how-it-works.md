# How EMART Works

## The EMART Process - Step by Step

### Step 1: Find Your Item
Browse international marketplaces like Mercari Japan, Rakuten, Yahoo! Auctions Japan, or Amazon Japan. Find the product you want to purchase.

### Step 2: Submit Purchase Request
Copy the product URL and paste it into your EMART account. Add any special instructions or requirements. Our system will calculate estimated costs.

### Step 3: We Purchase For You
Once you approve the quote and pay the proxy fee, our team purchases the item on your behalf using local payment methods. We handle all communication with the seller in their native language.

### Step 4: Item Arrives at Our Warehouse
The seller ships the item to our secure warehouse in Japan. We receive, verify, and log your package. You can request photos or inspection at this stage.

### Step 5: Choose Shipping Method
Once your item arrives, you can:
- Ship immediately
- Wait and consolidate with other purchases
- Request repackaging to reduce size/weight

### Step 6: International Shipping
We ship your package internationally using your chosen carrier (EMS, DHL, FedEx, etc.). You receive a tracking number to monitor delivery.

### Step 7: Delivery to Your Door
Your package is delivered directly to your address. Enjoy your international purchase!

## Key Features

### Package Consolidation
Combine multiple purchases into one shipment to save significantly on international shipping costs. All items purchased within 60 days can be consolidated.

### Storage Period
Free storage at our warehouse for up to 60 days from the first item's arrival. Extended storage available for a small fee.

### Package Inspection
Request photos or detailed inspection of your items before international shipping. We verify condition, authenticity, and completeness.

### Repackaging Service
Reduce shipping costs by removing excess packaging, boxes, or bundling items more efficiently.

## Account Dashboard

Your EMART dashboard shows:
- Pending purchase requests
- Items in warehouse
- Packages in transit
- Order history
- Total estimated costs
- Available shipping options
