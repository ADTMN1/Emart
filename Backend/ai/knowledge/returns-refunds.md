# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Important Understanding

EMART is a **proxy shopping service**. We purchase items on your behalf from third-party sellers. Our return and refund policy is governed by:
1. The original seller's return policy
2. Whether the item has left our warehouse
3. The reason for the return

## Refund Eligibility

### ✓ Eligible for Full Refund

**Before Purchase**
- You cancel before we complete the purchase
- Seller cancels or refuses to sell
- Item is no longer available
- We cannot complete purchase for technical reasons

**After Purchase - Before Warehouse**
- Seller sends wrong item
- Item significantly different from description
- Item arrives damaged to warehouse
- Item is counterfeit (verified by our inspection)
- Item missing major components

**After Purchase - At Warehouse**
- Severe damage discovered during inspection
- Item is counterfeit (confirmed through detailed inspection)
- Completely different product than ordered

### ✗ NOT Eligible for Refund

**Change of Mind**
- You no longer want the item
- You found it cheaper elsewhere
- Buyer's remorse

**After International Shipping**
- Package has been shipped internationally
- Item was as described but you don't like it
- Minor cosmetic differences from listing
- Color/size is slightly different than expected (unless grossly misrepresented)

**Third-Party Issues**
- Customs fees or duties
- Delivery delays by courier
- Package lost by courier (insurance claim instead)
- Import restrictions in your country

## Refund Amounts

### Full Refund Includes
- Item purchase price
- Domestic shipping fee (if item wasn't shipped)
- Proxy service fee (if we couldn't complete purchase)

### Partial Refund Scenarios

**Seller Accepts Return**
- Item purchase price: Refunded after seller confirms
- Domestic shipping: NOT refunded (already used)
- Proxy fee: NOT refunded (service already rendered)
- Return shipping to seller: Customer pays

**Item Damaged in Warehouse**
- Full refund if damage occurred at warehouse
- Insurance claim if damage occurred during domestic shipping
- Photo evidence required

### Non-Refundable Fees
- International shipping (if package already shipped)
- Photo inspection fees (service already rendered)
- Repackaging fees (service already rendered)
- Extended storage fees (storage already provided)
- Payment processing fees (3% for international transactions)
- Return shipping costs

## Return Process

### Step 1: Contact Support Immediately
- Email: support@emart.com
- Provide order number and detailed reason
- Include photos if item is damaged or wrong
- Do this within 48 hours of warehouse arrival

### Step 2: Evaluation
- Our team reviews your case
- May request additional photos
- Checks against item listing
- Determines eligibility
- Response within 24-48 hours

### Step 3: Resolution Options

**Option A: Return to Seller**
- Contact seller on your behalf
- Seller must agree to accept return
- You pay return shipping within Japan (¥700-¥1,500)
- Refund processed after seller confirms receipt
- Timeline: 1-2 weeks

**Option B: Keep with Partial Refund**
- Negotiate partial refund with seller
- You keep the item
- Reduced cost compensation
- Faster resolution

**Option C: Disposal**
- If item cannot be returned or shipped
- No refund if you choose disposal
- Free disposal service

**Option D: Ship Anyway**
- Ship item internationally despite issues
- No refund, but item delivered
- You may be able to return to seller directly (seller's policy applies)

### Step 4: Refund Processing
Once approved:
- Refund initiated within 3-5 business days
- Payment method: Same as original payment
- Processing time varies by payment method (see Payments document)

## Seller Return Policies

Since EMART buys from third-party sellers, their policies vary:

### Mercari Japan
- Returns only if item significantly misrepresented
- Must be reported within 3 days of delivery
- Seller has discretion to accept/decline
- Refund through platform if seller agrees

### Rakuten
- Varies by individual shop
- Usually 7-14 days return window
- Some items non-returnable (opened media, custom items)
- Return shipping usually paid by buyer

### Yahoo! Auctions Japan
- Auction sales are typically final
- Returns only for misrepresentation or damage
- Must contact seller directly
- Success depends on seller cooperation

### Amazon Japan
- Generally good return policy (30 days)
- Easier to process returns
- Amazon may accept returns directly
- More customer-friendly

## Special Cases

### Counterfeit Items
- Zero tolerance for counterfeits
- Full refund including all fees
- We report seller to platform
- Free disposal or return

### Damaged During Domestic Shipping
- File claim with domestic carrier
- Usually covered by seller's shipping insurance
- Full refund once claim approved
- Timeline: 2-4 weeks

### Wrong Size/Color (Customer Error)
- Not eligible for refund through EMART
- You may contact seller directly
- Success depends on seller's goodwill
- Return shipping costs apply

### Wrong Size/Color (Seller Error)
- Seller sent wrong variant
- Eligible for return/refund
- We handle communication with seller
- Seller typically covers return shipping

## Timeframes

### Request Deadline
- Must request return within **48 hours** of warehouse arrival
- After 48 hours, cases reviewed individually
- After international shipping, returns generally not possible

### Processing Time
- Initial review: 24-48 hours
- Seller communication: 3-7 days
- Refund processing: 3-5 days after approval
- Total timeline: 1-3 weeks typically

## How to Minimize Return Issues

### Before Ordering
1. **Read listing carefully** - Check all details, measurements, condition
2. **Request photos** - Ask seller questions through EMART
3. **Check seller ratings** - Choose reputable sellers
4. **Understand item condition** - New, used, junk, etc.
5. **Verify compatibility** - Size, voltage, region locks

### Use Our Services
1. **Photo Inspection** - ¥200-¥500 catches issues before shipping
2. **Detailed Inspection** - We verify authenticity and condition
3. **Measurements** - Request specific measurements

## Dispute Resolution

If you disagree with our decision:
1. Provide additional evidence
2. Escalate to senior support team
3. We review again with fresh perspective
4. Final decision made within 5 business days
