# EMART Proxy Fees

## Proxy Service Fee Structure

EMART charges a service fee for purchasing items on your behalf. This fee covers:
- Purchasing the item with local payment methods
- Communication with sellers
- Package receiving and verification
- Warehouse processing and storage (up to 60 days)
- Customer support

## Fee Calculation

Our proxy fee is calculated based on the item's purchase price:

| Item Price (JPY) | Proxy Fee |
|------------------|-----------|
| ¥0 - ¥5,000 | ¥500 per item |
| ¥5,001 - ¥10,000 | ¥800 per item |
| ¥10,001 - ¥30,000 | 8% of item price |
| ¥30,001 - ¥100,000 | 7% of item price |
| ¥100,001+ | 6% of item price |

**Minimum fee per transaction: ¥500**

## What's Included

The proxy fee includes:
- ✓ Purchase service
- ✓ Communication with seller
- ✓ Payment processing
- ✓ Basic package receiving
- ✓ 60 days free warehouse storage
- ✓ Package logging and inventory management
- ✓ Basic customer support

## What's NOT Included

The proxy fee does NOT include:
- ✗ Item purchase price
- ✗ Japanese domestic shipping (from seller to our warehouse)
- ✗ International shipping costs
- ✗ Optional inspection fees
- ✗ Repackaging fees
- ✗ Extended storage beyond 60 days
- ✗ Import duties and taxes in your country

## Additional Service Fees

### Photo Inspection
- Basic photos (3-5 images): ¥200 per package
- Detailed inspection with 10+ photos: ¥500 per package

### Repackaging
- Basic repackaging: ¥300 per package
- Advanced repackaging (remove multiple boxes): ¥600 per package

### Extended Storage
- Days 61-90: ¥100 per day
- Days 91+: ¥200 per day

### Package Consolidation
- Free for up to 5 items
- ¥200 for each additional item beyond 5

## Payment Terms

- Proxy fees must be paid upfront before purchase
- We accept major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers
- All fees are quoted in Japanese Yen (¥) but can be paid in your local currency
- Exchange rates are updated daily
