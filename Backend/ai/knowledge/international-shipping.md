# International Shipping from EMART

## Shipping Methods

EMART offers multiple international shipping options with different speeds and costs:

### EMS (Express Mail Service)
- **Speed**: 3-7 business days
- **Tracking**: Full tracking included
- **Insurance**: Up to ¥20,000 included
- **Best For**: Fast delivery, valuable items
- **Cost Range**: ¥2,000 - ¥15,000+ depending on weight and destination

### DHL Express
- **Speed**: 2-5 business days
- **Tracking**: Real-time tracking
- **Insurance**: Up to $100 included
- **Best For**: Fastest delivery, urgent items
- **Cost Range**: ¥3,000 - ¥20,000+ depending on weight and destination

### FedEx International Priority
- **Speed**: 3-6 business days
- **Tracking**: Full tracking included
- **Insurance**: Up to $100 included
- **Best For**: Reliable fast shipping
- **Cost Range**: ¥3,000 - ¥18,000+ depending on weight and destination

### Airmail (Standard)
- **Speed**: 10-21 business days
- **Tracking**: Basic tracking
- **Insurance**: Optional (additional fee)
- **Best For**: Budget shipping, non-urgent items
- **Cost Range**: ¥1,500 - ¥8,000+ depending on weight and destination

### SAL (Surface Air Lifted)
- **Speed**: 15-30 business days
- **Tracking**: Basic tracking
- **Insurance**: Optional (additional fee)
- **Best For**: Budget shipping for heavier items
- **Cost Range**: ¥1,200 - ¥6,000+ depending on weight and destination

### Sea Mail (Surface)
- **Speed**: 1-3 months
- **Tracking**: Limited or no tracking
- **Insurance**: Optional (additional fee)
- **Best For**: Very heavy items, lowest cost, non-urgent
- **Cost Range**: ¥800 - ¥4,000+ depending on weight

## Cost Factors

International shipping costs depend on:

1. **Weight**: Heavier packages cost more
2. **Dimensions**: Volumetric weight may apply for large, light packages
3. **Destination Country**: Different zones have different rates
4. **Shipping Method**: Express services cost more than standard
5. **Declared Value**: Higher value items may cost more to insure

## Volumetric Weight

For large but lightweight packages, carriers use volumetric weight:
**Volumetric Weight (kg) = (Length × Width × Height in cm) ÷ 6,000**

The higher of actual weight or volumetric weight is used for shipping calculation.

## Shipping Zones

Countries are grouped into zones affecting price:

- **Zone 1**: Asia (Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Southeast Asia)
- **Zone 2**: Oceania (Australia, New Zealand)
- **Zone 3**: North America (USA, Canada)
- **Zone 4**: Europe
- **Zone 5**: Middle East, Africa, South America

## Insurance

- **Included**: Basic insurance included with EMS, DHL, FedEx
- **Additional Coverage**: Available for high-value items
- **Cost**: Approximately 1-2% of declared value
- **Maximum**: Varies by carrier (up to ¥200,000 or $2,000)

## Customs and Duties

**Important**: You (the customer) are responsible for customs duties, taxes, and fees in your country.

- EMART declares packages accurately and honestly
- We cannot mark packages as "gift" or undervalue items
- Each country has different duty-free thresholds
- You may be contacted by customs for payment

## Delivery Time Estimates

Actual delivery time depends on:
- Chosen shipping method
- Destination country
- Customs processing time
- Local delivery efficiency
- Holidays and peak seasons

## Tracking

All shipments include tracking numbers. You can track packages through:
- EMART dashboard
- Carrier's website
- Universal tracking services

## Prohibited Items for Shipping

Some items cannot be shipped internationally:
- Lithium batteries (restrictions apply)
- Liquids over 100ml (restrictions apply)
- Aerosols and pressurized containers
- Flammable materials
- Weapons or weapon-like items
- Counterfeit goods
- Perishable foods

## Tips to Reduce Shipping Costs

1. **Consolidate**: Combine multiple items into one shipment
2. **Repackage**: Remove excess packaging to reduce weight/size
3. **Choose Slower Methods**: Airmail or SAL for non-urgent items
4. **Avoid Peak Seasons**: Shipping costs may increase during holidays
5. **Optimize Weight**: Stay just under weight tier thresholds when possible
