# EMART Company Information

## About EMART

EMART is a global proxy shopping platform that bridges the gap between international marketplaces and customers worldwide. We specialize in helping customers purchase products from Japanese and other international marketplaces that typically don't ship internationally.

## Mission

Our mission is to make global shopping accessible to everyone by eliminating geographical barriers, language obstacles, and payment restrictions.

## What We Do

- **Proxy Shopping Service**: We purchase items on your behalf from international marketplaces
- **Package Consolidation**: Combine multiple purchases into one shipment to save on shipping costs
- **International Shipping**: Ship your purchases directly to your doorstep anywhere in the world
- **Warehousing**: Secure storage for your packages before international shipping
- **Quality Inspection**: Optional package inspection and photos before shipping

## Service Coverage

- **Primary Focus**: Japanese marketplaces (Mercari, Rakuten, Yahoo! Auctions Japan, Amazon Japan)
- **Available Worldwide**: We ship to most countries globally
- **24/7 Support**: Customer service available around the clock

## Why Choose EMART

1. **No Language Barriers**: We handle all communication with sellers in their native language
2. **Local Payment Methods**: We use local payment methods so you don't need Japanese credit cards or bank accounts
3. **Package Consolidation**: Save money by combining multiple items into one shipment
4. **Secure Warehousing**: Your items are safely stored until you're ready to ship
5. **Transparent Pricing**: Clear breakdown of all costs with no hidden fees
6. **Customer Protection**: We verify purchases and inspect packages before international shipping
