# EMART Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Questions

### What is EMART?
EMART is a proxy shopping service that helps you buy products from Japanese marketplaces (Mercari, Rakuten, Yahoo! Auctions, Amazon Japan, etc.) and ships them internationally to your door.

### How does proxy shopping work?
You find an item in Japan, we buy it on your behalf using local payment methods, receive it at our warehouse, and then ship it internationally to you.

### Which marketplaces do you support?
- Mercari Japan
- Rakuten
- Yahoo! Auctions Japan
- Yahoo! Shopping Japan
- Amazon Japan
- Other Japanese online stores (case-by-case)

### Do I need a Japanese address or payment method?
No! That's the point of using EMART. We provide the Japanese address and use local payment methods.

## Costs and Fees

### What fees does EMART charge?
- **Proxy fee**: ¥500 to 8% depending on item price
- Additional optional services: Photo inspection, repackaging, etc.
- See the Proxy Fees knowledge document for complete breakdown

### What about shipping costs?
You pay two types of shipping:
- **Domestic** (seller to our warehouse): Set by seller
- **International** (our warehouse to you): Based on weight, destination, and shipping method

### Are there hidden fees?
No. All fees are clearly displayed in your quote before you pay.

### Do I pay customs duties?
Yes, customs duties and taxes in your country are your responsibility. EMART cannot predict these costs as they vary by country and item.

## Ordering Process

### How do I place an order?
1. Find item on Japanese marketplace
2. Copy the URL
3. Paste into your EMART dashboard
4. We calculate costs and send you a quote
5. Approve quote and pay
6. We purchase the item

### How long does it take?
- Purchase: 1-2 days
- Domestic shipping to warehouse: 2-7 days
- Processing at warehouse: 1-2 days
- International shipping: 3-30 days (depending on method)
- **Total: 1-6 weeks typically**

### Can I order multiple items?
Yes! You can order as many items as you want. Consider consolidating them into one package to save on international shipping.

### What if the item sells out?
If the item is no longer available, we cannot purchase it and will refund you the full amount.

### Can you negotiate price with sellers?
For auction sites like Yahoo! Auctions, we can bid up to your maximum price. For fixed-price items, we cannot negotiate.

## Shipping

### What shipping methods are available?
- EMS (3-7 days)
- DHL (2-5 days)
- FedEx (3-6 days)
- Airmail (10-21 days)
- SAL (15-30 days)
- Sea Mail (1-3 months)

### How much does international shipping cost?
Depends on weight, dimensions, destination, and method. Examples:
- 1kg to USA via EMS: ~¥3,500
- 2kg to UK via DHL: ~¥7,500
- See International Shipping document for detailed pricing

### Do you ship to my country?
We ship to most countries worldwide. Some restricted countries include those under international sanctions.

### What if my package is lost?
All express shipping includes tracking and insurance. If lost, we file an insurance claim for you.

### Can you mark items as "gift" or lower the value?
No. We must declare items accurately and honestly. Asking us to misdeclare is customs fraud.

## Returns and Refunds

### Can I return items?
Returns depend on the original seller's policy and must be requested within 48 hours of warehouse arrival. See Returns and Refunds document for details.

### What if item is damaged?
If damaged during domestic shipping or at our warehouse, we handle it with the carrier or seller for refund/replacement.

### What if I don't like the item?
Change of mind is generally not eligible for refund, especially after international shipping.

### How long do refunds take?
3-14 business days depending on payment method.

## Prohibited Items

### What items can't you ship?
- Weapons, explosives, hazardous materials
- Live animals or plants
- Counterfeit goods
- Illegal items
- Some liquids and batteries
- See Prohibited Items document for complete list

### Can you ship food?
Packaged snacks are usually okay. Fresh food, meat, and dairy are prohibited.

### Can you ship electronics?
Yes, but items with lithium batteries have restrictions.

### Can you ship alcohol?
Very restricted. Most countries prohibit or heavily restrict alcohol imports.

## Success Tips

### How to save money?
1. Consolidate multiple purchases
2. Use repackaging to reduce weight
3. Choose slower shipping methods for non-urgent items
4. Wait for seller promotions
5. Avoid peak shipping seasons

### How to avoid issues?
1. Read listings carefully
2. Check seller ratings
3. Request photos before international shipping
4. Choose reputable sellers
5. Research your country's import restrictions

### How to speed up delivery?
1. Use express shipping (EMS, DHL)
2. Ship immediately (don't wait for consolidation)
3. Ensure customs forms are accurate
4. Choose items that are in stock
