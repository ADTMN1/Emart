## What You Pay For

Your EMART payment covers:

### 1. Item Purchase Price
The cost of the item as listed by the seller

### 2. Domestic Shipping
Shipping from seller to EMART warehouse in Japan

### 3. Proxy Service Fee
EMART's fee for purchasing on your behalf (see Proxy Fees document)

### 4. International Shipping
Shipping from our warehouse to your address (chosen by you)

### 5. Optional Services (if selected)
- Photo inspection
- Repackaging
- Extended storage
- Additional insurance

### What's NOT Included
- Import duties and taxes in your destination country
- Currency conversion fees (if charged by your bank)
- Bank transfer fees
- Cryptocurrency network fees

## Payment Process

### Step 1: Purchase Request
Submit the item URL and your shipping address. We calculate costs.

### Step 2: Quote Approval
Review the detailed quote showing:
- Item price
- Domestic shipping
- Proxy fee
- Estimated international shipping
- Total cost

### Step 3: Payment
Pay the quoted amount using your preferred payment method.

### Step 4: Purchase
Once payment clears, we purchase the item immediately.

### Step 5: Warehouse Arrival
Item arrives at warehouse. International shipping is already paid (if you chose to pay upfront).

### Alternative: Split Payment
- Pay for proxy service + domestic shipping upfront
- Pay for international shipping later after item arrives and actual weight is confirmed

## Refunds

### Eligible for Refund
- Seller cancels or doesn't ship
- Item is significantly different from description
- Item arrives damaged (before leaving our warehouse)
- We cannot complete purchase for any reason

### Not Eligible for Refund
- You change your mind after purchase
- Item is as described but you don't like it
- Buyer's remorse
- Customs fees or duties
- Delivery delays beyond our control
- Minor cosmetic differences from listing

### Refund Processing Time
- Credit/Debit Card: 5-10 business days
- PayPal: 3-5 business days
- Bank Transfer: 7-14 business days
- Cryptocurrency: 1-3 business days

### Refund Amount
- Full refund of item price if not purchased
- Proxy fee: Refunded if we cannot complete purchase
- Domestic shipping: Refunded if seller cancels before shipping
- International shipping: Refunded if package never leaves warehouse
- Payment processing fees may not be refundable

## Payment Issues

### Declined Payment
Common reasons:
- Insufficient funds
- Card doesn't support international transactions
- Bank fraud prevention
- Incorrect card details

**Solution**: Contact your bank or try alternative payment method

### Currency Conversion Differences
Small differences between quoted price and charged amount may occur due to:
- Real-time exchange rate fluctuations
- Bank conversion fees
- Payment processor fees

### Chargebacks
If you initiate a chargeback:
- We will investigate and provide evidence
- Your account may be suspended pending resolution
- If chargeback is unjustified, account may be permanently closed
- Please contact support before initiating chargeback

## Payment FAQ

**Q: When do I pay?**
A: Before we purchase the item on your behalf.

**Q: Can I pay in installments?**
A: Not at this time. Full payment required upfront.

**Q: Are there hidden fees?**
A: No. All fees are disclosed in the quote before payment.

**Q: Do you store my card information?**
A: We use secure payment processors. Your full card number is never stored on our servers.

**Q: Can I get an invoice?**
A: Yes. All transactions include a detailed invoice sent to your email.

**Q: What if the item costs more than quoted?**
A: We confirm exact price before purchase. If higher, we contact you for approval or refund the difference.
