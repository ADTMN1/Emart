# Japanese Domestic Shipping

## What is Domestic Shipping?

Domestic shipping is the cost for the seller to ship the item from their location in Japan to our EMART warehouse in Japan. This is separate from international shipping.

## Cost Information

Domestic shipping costs vary depending on:
- **Seller's Location**: Distance from seller to our warehouse
- **Package Size and Weight**: Larger/heavier items cost more
- **Shipping Method**: Standard post vs. courier service
- **Seller's Preference**: Each seller sets their own shipping rates

## Typical Domestic Shipping Costs

### Small Items (Books, Accessories, Electronics)
- Japan Post Regular Mail: ¥200 - ¥500
- Japan Post Compact: ¥300 - ¥600
- Yamato Takkyubin (Courier): ¥600 - ¥900

### Medium Items (Clothing, Shoes, Small Appliances)
- Japan Post Regular: ¥500 - ¥1,000
- Yamato Takkyubin: ¥900 - ¥1,500

### Large Items (Bags, Larger Electronics)
- Japan Post: ¥1,000 - ¥2,000
- Yamato Takkyubin: ¥1,500 - ¥3,000

### Very Large/Heavy Items (Furniture, Large Appliances)
- Special freight shipping: ¥3,000 - ¥10,000+

## Who Pays Domestic Shipping?

**You (the customer) pay for domestic shipping.** This cost is added to your total purchase quote. The domestic shipping fee is shown on the product listing by the seller, and we include it in your purchase estimate.

## Common Domestic Carriers

1. **Japan Post** - Most economical, slower but reliable
2. **Yamato Transport (Kuroneko)** - Fast and reliable courier
3. **Sagawa Express** - Similar to Yamato
4. **Yu-Pack** - Japan Post's parcel service

## Free Domestic Shipping

Some Japanese sellers offer free domestic shipping within Japan. When a seller offers free shipping, you won't be charged for domestic shipping to our warehouse.

## Tracking

Most domestic shipments include tracking. We monitor all incoming packages and update your dashboard when items arrive at our warehouse.

## Delivery Time to Warehouse

- **Same city**: 1-2 days
- **Different prefecture**: 2-4 days
- **Remote areas**: 3-7 days

## Important Notes

- Domestic shipping is charged by the seller, not EMART
- We cannot control or negotiate domestic shipping prices
- Some sellers only ship via specific carriers
- Domestic shipping is separate from international shipping costs
- Large or heavy items may require special handling
