# Prohibited and Restricted Items

## Items We Cannot Purchase or Ship

For legal, safety, and shipping regulation reasons, EMART cannot handle certain items.

## Absolutely Prohibited

These items cannot be purchased or shipped under any circumstances:

### Illegal Items
- Counterfeit goods or replicas
- Pirated media or software
- Stolen goods
- Items violating intellectual property rights
- Illegal drugs or drug paraphernalia
- Child exploitation materials

### Weapons and Dangerous Goods
- Firearms, ammunition, or gun parts
- Explosives, fireworks, or flammable materials
- Pepper spray, mace, or tear gas
- Knives with blades over 6cm (strict rules)
- Sword, daggers, or combat weapons
- Stun guns or tasers

### Hazardous Materials
- Radioactive materials
- Toxic chemicals or poisons
- Biological hazards or medical waste
- Infectious materials
- Strong acids or bases
- Mercury-containing items

### Living Things
- Live animals or insects
- Live plants or seeds (most countries)
- Biological specimens
- Animal products from endangered species

## Heavily Restricted Items

These items have strict limitations and may require special approval:

### Batteries and Electronics
**Lithium Batteries**
- Loose lithium batteries: Prohibited in most shipping methods
- Devices with installed batteries: Allowed with restrictions
- Power banks: Maximum 100Wh, must be in original packaging
- E-cigarettes: Prohibited by most carriers

**Electronics**
- ✓ Smartphones and tablets: Generally allowed
- ✓ Laptops: Allowed with restrictions
- ⚠️ Vintage electronics: May contain prohibited materials
- ⚠️ Large batteries: Require special handling

### Liquids and Cosmetics
**Limitations:**
- Maximum 100ml per container for air shipping
- Alcohol content restrictions (no more than 70% alcohol)
- Pressurized containers limited

**Examples:**
- ⚠️ Perfume: Small quantities only (under 100ml)
- ⚠️ Nail polish: Restricted
- ⚠️ Aerosol sprays: Generally prohibited
- ✓ Solid cosmetics: Usually allowed

### Alcohol and Tobacco
**Alcohol:**
- Cannot ship to many countries
- Maximum alcohol content restrictions
- Quantity limits apply
- Import permits required in most countries
- High customs duties expected

**Tobacco:**
- Cigarettes: Heavily restricted, high duties
- Vape products: Prohibited by most carriers
- Tobacco leaves: Import permits required

### Food Items
**Perishable Foods:**
- ✗ Fresh fruits and vegetables: Prohibited
- ✗ Fresh meat or seafood: Prohibited
- ✗ Dairy products: Prohibited

**Non-Perishable Foods:**
- ✓ Packaged snacks: Generally allowed
- ✓ Candy and sweets: Allowed
- ⚠️ Contains meat/dairy: May be restricted
- ⚠️ Supplements: May require permits

**Special Cases:**
- ⚠️ Coffee and tea: Usually allowed
- ⚠️ Spices: May require phytosanitary certificate
- ⚠️ Seeds: Usually prohibited

### Medicine and Supplements
**Prescription Medication:**
- ✗ Cannot purchase on your behalf
- ✗ Cannot ship internationally without permits
- Most countries prohibit importing prescription drugs

**Over-the-Counter Medicine:**
- ⚠️ Limited quantities may be allowed
- ⚠️ Must declare to customs
- ⚠️ Some countries prohibit certain ingredients

**Supplements:**
- ⚠️ Usually allowed in small quantities
- ⚠️ Some ingredients prohibited in certain countries
- ⚠️ Declare on customs forms

### Adult Content
**Prohibited:**
- ✗ Pornographic materials (strictly enforced in Japan)
- ✗ Sexually explicit items
- ✗ Items depicting minors in any sexual context

**Restricted:**
- ⚠️ Artistic nude photography: Case-by-case basis
- ⚠️ Adult toys: Restricted by some carriers and countries

### Branded and Luxury Goods
**High Risk for Counterfeits:**
- ⚠️ Designer handbags: Require verification
- ⚠️ Luxury watches: Require authenticity check
- ⚠️ Designer clothing: Inspected carefully
- ⚠️ Branded electronics: Verified as genuine

**We Require:**
- Detailed photos before purchase
- May request additional verification
- May refuse if authenticity doubtful
- You assume risk for customs seizure of counterfeits

## Country-Specific Restrictions

### United States
- Kinder Surprise eggs: Prohibited
- Certain meats and dairy: Prohibited
- Cuban products: Restricted

### European Union
- Items not meeting CE standards: Restricted
- Certain plant products: Require phytosanitary certificate
- Some cosmetics: Require EU compliance

### Australia
- Strict biosecurity laws: No food items, wood, seeds
- Tobacco: Very high duties
- Therapeutic goods: Strict restrictions

### Canada
- Consumer products: Must meet Canadian standards
- Used items: May require additional documentation

### United Kingdom
- Post-Brexit restrictions apply
- VAT applies to all imports
- CE marking requirements

## Size and Weight Restrictions

### Maximum Dimensions
- **Standard Shipping**: Up to 160cm (length + width + height)
- **Oversized Items**: 160-200cm require special handling (additional fees)
- **Cannot Ship**: Over 200cm total dimensions

### Maximum Weight
- **Standard Shipping**: Up to 30kg
- **Heavy Items**: 30-50kg require special handling (additional fees)
- **Cannot Ship**: Over 50kg (freight shipping required)

## What Happens if You Order Prohibited Items

### Before Purchase
- We review all purchase requests
- Prohibited items are rejected immediately
- Full refund issued
- Explanation provided

### After Purchase (Discovered at Warehouse)
- Item cannot be shipped internationally
- Options: Return to seller (fees apply) or disposal
- Refund depends on circumstances
- You pay for return/disposal

### Customs Seizure
- If customs seizes prohibited items
- No refund possible
- You are responsible for any penalties
- Item is lost

## How to Check if Item is Allowed

### Before Ordering
1. **Check item description carefully**
2. **Review this prohibited items list**
3. **Check your country's customs regulations**
4. **Ask EMART support if unsure** - support@emart.com

### Resources
- Your country's customs website
- International Air Transport Association (IATA) guidelines
- Universal Postal Union restrictions
- Carrier-specific prohibited items lists

## Tips for Compliance

✓ **Always declare items accurately** - Do not ask us to misdeclare
✓ **Research your country's rules** - Each country is different
✓ **Ask before ordering** - Better to check than lose money
✓ **Be honest in purchase requests** - Helps us help you
✓ **Check carrier restrictions** - DHL, FedEx, EMS have different rules

⚠️ **Never ask us to:**
- Mark items as gift when they're not
- Undervalue items on customs forms
- Mislabel contents
- Hide items in other packages

## Consequences of Ordering Prohibited Items

- Order cancellation and refund (minus fees if already purchased)
- Item disposal at your expense
- Customs seizure with no refund
- Account suspension for repeated violations
- Legal penalties in severe cases (weapons, drugs, counterfeits)
